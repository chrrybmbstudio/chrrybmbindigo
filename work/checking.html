checking hub redesign

credit union of georgia — simpler IA, clearer plan compare, accessible CTAs, and search hygiene.

uxiaseoaccessibilityanalytics
overview

problem

Users struggled to compare checking options and choose a path. The previous page buried CTAs, mixed headings, and repeated content, which slowed decision-making.

goal

Reduce friction to account selection and improve findability for checking plans.

approach

  • Restructure page; introduce a clear plan matrix with consistent labels.
  • Clarify CTA hierarchy (primary apply, secondary learn) with obvious exits.
  • Tighten microcopy & metadata; normalize H1–H3; improve accessibility.
  • Monitor via GA; audit backlinks in SEMrush; ship disavow through Search Console.
live site back to projects

ux & ia

  • Reorganized content into a clear plan matrix with consistent labels.
  • Added CTA hierarchy (primary apply, secondary learn) with obvious exits.
  • Normalized H1–H3 structure; removed duplicate headings.
  • Improved focus states, contrast, and motion sensitivity.

content & seo hygiene

  • Trimmed marketing filler; tightened microcopy for clarity.
  • Alt text, descriptive links, and purposeful metadata.
  • Submitted sitemap & ensured robots directives were sane.

search quality

  • SEMrush backlink audit → compiled disavow list to reduce toxic links.
  • Shipped disavow file via Google Search Console.
  • Monitored keyword movement and indexed coverage weekly.

measurement

  • Weekly Google Analytics snapshots: sessions, time on page, exit rate, and conversion events.
  • Compared pre/post windows and annotated launch in GA for clarity.
